What We Believe

Five things we hold to.

  • Housing is the missing piece. Treatment works far better when there is somewhere stable to go afterward.
  • Structure is a kindness. Curfews and testing are not punishment. They are what makes the house safe enough to be worth living in.
  • Dignity is not a reward. Nobody earns being treated like a person. That is the starting condition.
  • Any path counts. AA, NA, church, therapy, medication-assisted treatment, or your own thing — we support whatever is keeping you sober.
  • We stay in our lane. We are a home, not a clinic. We say so plainly and we do not pretend otherwise.

Who We Are For

Men who are ready to do the work.

Coming out of treatment. Coming home from incarceration. Coming off a couch you have worn out your welcome on. If you are serious about staying sober and willing to live by house rules, there is a place for you here.
