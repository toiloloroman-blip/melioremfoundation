Where support goes

Meliorem Foundation supports the work of the house — furnishing rooms, keeping beds available for men who arrive with nothing, and covering the gaps that come up when someone is starting over with a duffel bag.

If you would rather give something than money, this is the list we actually keep:

  • Twin and full bed frames
  • New mattresses
  • Sheets, pillows, blankets
  • Towels and bath mats
  • Dressers and nightstands
  • Kitchen table and chairs
  • Pots, pans, dishes, utensils
  • Microwave, coffee maker, toaster
  • Couches and living room seating
  • Lamps and floor lighting
  • Cleaning supplies and paper goods
  • Gift cards for groceries or gas

Gently used furniture is welcome and genuinely appreciated. For health reasons we can only accept new mattresses, pillows and bedding.
